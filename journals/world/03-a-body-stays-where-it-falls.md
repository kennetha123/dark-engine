# Journal world/03 — A body stays where it falls

Status: Decided.
Date: 2026-09-25. Depends on: `journals/world/01`, `journals/world/02`.

## Goal

Settle what death does: to the character, to the world, to the save, and to a co-op session.

## Decisions

**Death is an ending, not a loss.** It is one of the four ways a year ends
(`journals/world/04`). Nothing about it is presented as failure.

**Single player: death ends the run.** One life, one year. The save becomes a **chronicle** — the
year is sealed, readable, not continuable.

**Co-op: the year continues.** The dead player makes a new character and joins the same year. The
creation questions are asked again, and the new character wakes where their own background says —
which may be days away from the party.

**The death is persistent in the world.** The old character stays dead and stays *present*.

**The corpse stays where it fell.** Whatever they were carrying is still on it.

**Settlements bury their dead; the wild does not.** In a town the body is taken and buried. Out
on the road it is not. So towns stay livable and the roads between them slowly become a record of
everything that has gone wrong for this party.

**The church buries.** A sexton in every settlement, digging graves. That gives humanity's
religion a presence in every town without a single cathedral, by way of the most necessary job in
the world. It also puts the church directly on top of the dark path's supply.

**Burial is a service you can buy, or work you can do.** Pay and it is done properly. Do it
yourself and you carry the body — by hand if you have nothing, by cart if you have one.

**A corpse is a resource on the dark path.** Ambrose turns people; bodies are what he wants for
it. Graves are therefore worth robbing, and the sexton is either someone you help or someone you
steal from.

**Corpse handling reads three ways, from one system:**

| Allegiance | A body is |
|---|---|
| Humanity | a duty. Leaving one is a sin, and people notice |
| Dark | a resource. Burying one is waste |
| Neutral | a practical problem — bury it before it draws wolves or sickens the well |

**An unburied body can be raised.** In a world with necromancy, leaving your friend where they
fell means meeting them again — hostile, wearing your gear. The engine already has players and
NPCs sharing one actor model with only the controller differing, so this is a controller swap
rather than a new system.

That is what gives burial teeth. It stops being a courtesy and becomes a decision with a cost:
you are tired, it is getting dark, the body is heavy, and if you walk away you may have to kill
them twice.

**This applies to the hero party too.** If Rein's party dies at the Dark Lord's castle
(`journals/world/06`), four corpses lie on the floor and every rule above holds. They can be
carried home and buried. They can also be raised.

## Open questions

- **The burial verb.** The hotbar is full at eight. Either burial needs a spade you have to carry,
  or it is a contextual action on the body, or it is bare hands and slow. A required spade means
  parties that plan for death and parties that do not.
- **Decay.** Fresh, then bones, would tell the player how old a body is at a glance, and would
  stop four players over a year carpeting the map in corpses. Not settled.
- **Scavengers.** A body in the open drawing crows and wolves would make it a hazard and a lure,
  and give leaving one a visible consequence. Not settled.
- **Gear recovery.** The proposal is that equipment stays on the body and must be walked back
  for — long enough to matter at a day per city, short enough to attempt — and is gone if you take
  too long. Not settled.
- **Carts are a system, not a detail.** Carrying a body by hand is close to what the engine does
  today. A cart is a vehicle. It should not arrive through the burial conversation.

## Non-goals

- Revival of a dead player character. Death is an ending; the answer is a new character, not the
  old one back.

## Note

Players will bury their dead in the same place without being asked to. A party cemetery built
over 365 days should be allowed to happen.
