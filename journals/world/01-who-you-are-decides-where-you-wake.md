# Journal world/01 — Who you are decides where you wake

Status: Decided.
Date: 2026-09-25. Depends on: `journals/world/00`.

## Goal

Settle character creation: what the game asks, what the answers mean, and where each one puts the
player on day one.

## Decisions

**Creation asks about a life, not a class.** The model is Mount & Blade — questions about
childhood, station and temperament. The answers combine.

**The shape is station × leaning.** Two axes, combining, so a handful of questions produce many
outcomes:

- **Station** — noble, common, poor, lowborn (in service), elf, undead.
- **Leaning** — politics, knighthood, study, magic, trade, freedom, leisure, vengeance, faith,
  craft.

**Your answers decide where you wake.** There is no single starting town. Every settled node is
somebody's home:

| Background | Wakes at |
|---|---|
| Noble, drawn to politics | Gulard |
| Noble, drawn to knighthood | Ravingard |
| Noble, drawn to study | Green Town |
| Cheerful, happy childhood | Folkstagaard |
| Workaholic, loves money | Mirkish |
| Study, magical or research-minded | School of Mage Tower |
| Freedom and warfare | Livingard |
| Poor; parents killed young; turned toward darkness but still human | Birkstein |
| Elf | Erdlis |
| Undead and poor | Porchin |
| Undead and noble | Castle of Horror |
| Undead and lowborn | Dark Lord Castle, as a maid or butler |

**Background is a head start, not a cage.** Joining a role is gated on **stats**, not on class —
the mage slot wants a minimum magic attack, and so on. A character born to it already clears the
threshold. A character not born to it can train up to it. It is harder. It is never closed.

That makes a farmer who trains hard enough to stand where an archmage would have stood a better
story than a mage who was always going to. See `journals/world/07` for the stat system and
`journals/world/06` for the thresholds it gates.

**Every node should have a background.** A location nobody starts at is a location that does not
have to be fully built, and that is how set pieces get in. The table above leaves sixteen nodes
unclaimed.

## Open questions

**Proposed fills for the unclaimed nodes.** None of these are settled:

| Node | Proposed background |
|---|---|
| Sovern | Common + knighthood — trained in, did not inherit it. The merit route, against Ravingard's birth route |
| The forge | Common + craft — apprenticed to the legendary blacksmith |
| Centreau | Common, no strong leaning. A farmer. The plainest start there is |
| Velkstein | Born at the front. War is weather to you |
| Veterans' village | You start **old**, your war already behind you |
| Mariacth | Common + faith — came up through the church, not the sword |
| Tarniard | Common + pleasure — cook, painter, musician |
| Magnoria | Miner |
| Narimbia | Desert-born |

The Ravingard / Sovern split is the one worth taking first: *born to it* against *earned it*,
the same career, two completely different social experiences.

**Where a new co-op character spawns after a death.** Decided in `journals/world/03`: the creation
questions are asked again and the new character wakes where their own background says. Open: what
the party does about the distance, which at a day per city could be several days.

## Non-goals

- The wording of the creation questions. The structure is settled; the script is game content and
  belongs with `docs/cast/` conventions, not here.
- Race beyond elf, human and undead.
