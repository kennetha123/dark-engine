# Journal world/04 — Four ways a year ends

Status: Decided.
Date: 2026-09-25. Depends on: `journals/world/02`, `journals/world/03`, `journals/world/06`.

## Goal

Settle how the game ends, what the win condition is, and why a player would take the long route
when the short one is open from day one.

## Decisions

**Four endings, each tied to what you chose to be.**

| Ending | Reached by |
|---|---|
| **Your own death** | Dying. Your death is the story, not a failure screen |
| **The hero's death** | Taking the dark religion and killing Rein |
| **The Dark Lord's death** | Taking humanity and killing him |
| **The year runs out** | Staying neutral and living all 365 days |

**The win condition is open from day one.** The Dark Lord can be reached and fought at any time.
Nothing gates him but capability.

**The Dark Lord is the strongest thing in the world.** He mends reality, and holds magic, dark
power and immense physical strength together. In combat he cannot be defeated by anyone — except
the protagonist, or the player.

**He is not evil. He is bored.** He has challenged every kind of enemy and stopped caring. He is
waiting for somebody worth fighting, and he is too lazy to involve himself in politics, which is
why his two guardians run the faction between them.

**Four Relics of the Gods kill him:** a **sword**, **runes**, a **hammer**, and a **book**. They
are the ordinary route — how mortals do it.

**One relic and enough skill is also enough.** The **Sword of Saint** lies unguarded in **Castle
of Horror**. A skilled player can take it and beat him solo, in hours rather than minutes.

**The sword is lying there because he left it there.** A bored god does not misplace the weapon
that kills him. It is an invitation, and the speedrun is the first person in four hundred years to
accept it.

**Castle of Horror is the undead-noble starting location** (`journals/world/01`). So one
background wakes up in the same castle as the weapon that kills its own god.

**If the player stays out of the war for the whole year, the hero party gathers the relics and
finishes it.** Neutrality is not idleness — it is the one path where the world saves itself and
you watched. The world simulation already runs a year and produces a chronicle, so that ending
largely writes itself from simulation output.

**Rein and Shanya marry if the hero party wins.** That is the ending of their story, and it is the
reason Rein does not take a throne he has a claim to (`docs/cast/`).

**The relics map one to a hero:**

| Relic | Hero |
|---|---|
| Sword | Rein |
| Book | Shanya |
| Hammer | Porrega |
| Runes | Rowen |

Four relics, four heroes, four players.

**If the player takes the relics, the hero party attacks anyway and dies.** They are stubborn and
they will not stop. Their corpses lie in the Dark Lord's mansion, and the player hears the news.
Everything in `journals/world/03` then applies to those four bodies.

**That death must be a choice, not a trick.** The player is warned first — Porrega, the one who
looks after everybody, comes and asks for them back. The player says no. *Then* they die. That is
the difference between a twist and a decision.

## Open questions

- **The set needs a new name.** "The Four Runes" cannot contain an item called "the Runes."
  *The Four Relics* is used here as a placeholder. Not settled.
- **Is the Sword of Saint named after Ambrose?** He was a bishop, the greatest human who ever
  lived, and the sword that kills the Dark Lord carries a saint's title. Might be too neat. Might
  be the whole game.
- **Who gets the relics if the player is involved in the war but does not gather them?** The
  neutral case is settled. The involved-but-unsuccessful case is not.
- **What the ending is if a player filled a hero's slot and the party wins.** "Rein marries
  Shanya" cannot happen if Shanya never joined (`journals/world/06`).
- **Gandahl is a fifth route.** Recruiting him makes quick work of the Dark Lord, because he is
  anti-magic and the Dark Lord's power is largely magical. Whether an undead player can also
  recruit him is not settled. See `docs/cast/`.

## Non-goals

- Post-ending play. The year ends; the save becomes a record of it.
