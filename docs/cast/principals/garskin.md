## What they want

A flower garden. That is the whole of it.

He is the strongest physical thing alive, second only to the Dark Lord in power overall, and what
he wants is somewhere to grow flowers and an evening with something sad to watch.

He also wants the war not to happen. He does not frame that as politics. He frames it as people
he knows being killed by other people he knows.

## What they did

Four hundred years ago he fought in the first war between humans and undead. Nobody records where
he came from. He has no lineage anybody writes down — an unknown zombie who turned out to be
stronger than everything.

He found a dog in that war and kept it alive ever since.

He holds the second guardian's seat under a Dark Lord who cannot be bothered with governing, which
makes him one of the two people actually running the undead. Ambrose wants humans dead. Garskin
does not. Ambrose cannot beat him, and that fact — one zombie who would rather garden — is the only
reason there has not been a second war.

## What they know

- That Ambrose is not wrong about what was done to him, only about who to make pay for it.
- That the Dark Lord knows about the dog and has never said a word.
- That the theatre at Tarniard puts on something worth seeing in the autumn.
- Who in the undead ranks would move for Ambrose if the order came, and who would not.
- That he is what is holding it shut, and that it will not hold forever.

**To a human player** — he helps. He does not care that you are human, which is disorienting if
you came expecting a monster.

**To an undead player** — the same. He does not care that you are undead either. He is the peace
faction, and there should be a path through him rather than through Ambrose.

## Lines

*(unwritten)*

He should never explain himself. The player finds out what he is by watching what he does when he
thinks nobody is looking — a coin given away, a garden tended, a seat at the back of a theatre.
Nothing he says should do that work for him.

## Connections

- **The dog** — four hundred years old, alive, in his basement. Unrotting because Garskin takes
  its decay into himself, continuously, and has done since the first war. The link is to his life,
  not to distance. **It dies when he does, wherever it is.** It has no name yet, and it needs one,
  because a player reads it off a collar.
- **The Dark Lord** — knows about the dog. Has done nothing. Cannot be bothered.
- **Ambrose** — cannot beat him, and needs him gone.
- **Tarniard** — he sneaks into the theatre. Human territory. They would burn him if they knew.

## Killing him

Recorded fully in `journals/world/05`. In short: it destroys the undead peace faction, frees
Ambrose, starts the war, and rots the dog. The player must be able to learn all of this
beforehand — rumours in the neutral towns, the tended garden outside, **a dog barking from the
basement during the fight** — and must not be able to save the dog by any route.
